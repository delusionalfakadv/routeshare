Matching Algorithm: Mathematical Formulation

d = 2R · atan2( √[ sin²(Δlat/2) + cos(lat1)·cos(lat2)·sin²(Δlng/2) ], √[ 1 - a ] )
Detour = (Dist(Start ➔ Pickup) + Dist(Pickup ➔ Dropoff) + Dist(Dropoff ➔ End)) - Dist(Direct)
Constraint: Detour ≤ 4.0 km (Optimal Overlap Heuristic)
